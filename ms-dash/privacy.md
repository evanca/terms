## The Short Version

- Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play, and RevenueCat
  checks them. We never see your payment details.

  Aptabase, with no persistent identifier. You can turn them off in Settings.
- The game shows no ads and doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.

## What Stays on Your Device

The game saves these on your device:

- your game: the current level, high score, remaining lives, the random layout
  of your campaign, and a paused round so you can resume it;
- the levels you have earned with daily claims, when you last claimed, and your
  claim streak;
- your sound setting and, once anonymous gameplay statistics ship, your choice
  about sharing them;
- whether you have finished the free first level, whether a purchase is waiting
  for the store, and when the app last checked your subscription. These values
  don't prove a subscription; the store and RevenueCat decide that;
- the last copy of the update-check file described below, and which update
  prompt you dismissed.

The game doesn't upload these to a game server. Deleting the app removes them
from your device. Device backups may keep copies under your platform's backup
settings. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

## Information We Collect

### Purchases and Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

The app starts RevenueCat only when you try to open a level you haven't
earned yet. It then asks RevenueCat and the store for the available plans and your
subscription status. Purchases and restores go through the store. RevenueCat
and the store process your purchase history and subscription status to
validate purchases and decide your access. RevenueCat also gives us purchase
reports.

RevenueCat creates a random app user ID, which the app keeps on your device. We
don't send RevenueCat your name or email address, and the app turns off
RevenueCat's automatic collection of device identifiers. RevenueCat still
processes that app user ID and your purchase history.

You never enter payment card details in the app; the store handles payment.

### Anonymous Gameplay Statistics (Aptabase): planned, confirm before publishing

We plan to count how players move through the game, so we can see whether the
first levels and the subscription screen work. The app would send only these
8 events:

1. the first time the game opens;
2. level 1 started;
3. the first time you catch a cat with a power sweet;
4. level 1 cleared;
5. the subscription screen shown, with the reason: next level, locked level,
   milestone or play all;
6. "claim tomorrow" chosen;
7. the subscription screen closed;
8. level 5 cleared.

Each event carries the app version and the platform. The Aptabase SDK also
adds, on its own: the time, the operating system name and version, the
device's language and region setting, the app's build number, the SDK version,
whether it is a development build, and a random session ID. The session ID is
kept in memory only and replaced after an hour without events. Events wait on
your device until they are sent.

There is no persistent identifier, no cookie and no advertising identifier, so
these counts can't follow you from one day to the next and aren't used to
track you. Subscriptions and trials are not part of these counts; they stay in
RevenueCat.

You can turn this off in **Settings → Share anonymous gameplay stats**. It is
on by default.

### The Update Check

When the game starts and each time you return to it, it downloads a small file
from this website to see whether you need an update. Like any web request, this
shows the website's host, GitHub Pages, your IP address and basic request
details. The game sends nothing else with it. **[Confirm that the release build

### Development Diagnostics

Development builds can keep a short diagnostic record in memory while the game
runs. It never leaves the device, is discarded when the game closes, and is
switched off in the release build.
