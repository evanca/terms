# Ms. Dash Support

Need help? Email [info@happycode.studio](mailto:info@happycode.studio). Tell us
your device, its system version and what happened. Please don't send passwords
or payment details. The **Support** button in the game's Settings opens your
email app addressed to us; nothing is sent until you send it.

## How do I restore my subscription?

Open a level that needs a subscription, then tap **Restore purchases** on the
subscription screen. Use the same Apple or Google account you subscribed with.

## How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Your store manages subscriptions, not us:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
- **Android:** Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions →
  Subscriptions.

**Manage subscription** on the game's subscription screen opens the same page.
Deleting the game doesn't cancel a subscription.

## What can I play for free?

Level 1 is free. After that you can claim one more level each day, on the
**Your levels** map, and every level you earn stays unlocked. A new claim opens
at midnight UTC; the map shows when in your local time. If you miss a day, you
can still claim the next day, but missed days don't add up. A subscription
unlocks every level.

Please don't set your device's clock back. If the game sees the clock go
backwards, new claims wait until that time comes around again.

## Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There is no account or online backup, so we can't restore
your progress or earned levels if you delete the game or change devices. Your
device's own backup may bring them back, depending on its settings. A
subscription belongs to your store account and is restored through the
store.
