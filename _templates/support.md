## How do I restore a purchase?

Open the App and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in to the same Apple or
Google account you bought with.

## How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your store, not by us:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
- **Android:** Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions →
  Subscriptions.

Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
