# Community Cats — Photo Guidelines

Community Cats lets you nominate a real cat to appear on the TV in the Puking Cat
lobby, where every other player can see it. This page is the rules for that — what we
accept, what happens to a photo after you send it, and how to reach us.

Please read it before you submit. It is short on purpose.

## Every photo is reviewed by someone from the app team before it appears

Nothing you send goes live automatically. Someone from the app team looks at every
submission and decides whether it appears in the game. Most of the rules below exist so
that decision is predictable rather than mysterious.

Review is not instant. If your cat has not shown up yet, it is usually still in the
queue.

## What we accept

**Cats.** That is the whole feature. A photo of your cat, someone's cat you have
permission to submit, or a cat you look after.

**A photo you own.** You must have taken it yourself, or have the right to give it to
us to publish. Do not send a photo you found online, a photo from a breeder's website,
or a photo a friend took unless they are happy for it to appear in a game.

## What we do not accept

- **People.** No faces, no hands holding the cat, nobody in the background. This is the
  rule most submissions trip on. Crop tightly, or take another photo.
- **Other animals as the subject.** A dog asleep in the corner of the frame is fine; a
  photo that is mostly a dog is not a cat photo.
- **Logos, brands and recognisable products.** No visible packaging, no team shirts, no
  branded cat food bags, no other games' characters. We cannot publish someone else's
  trademark.
- **Text on the image.** No captions, no watermarks, no memes, no stickers. The cat's
  name goes in the form, not on the picture.
- **Anything unlawful, sexual, violent, hateful, or otherwise objectionable.** Also:
  nothing showing an animal being harmed, frightened, or mistreated.
- **Anything that identifies a place or a person** — a street sign, a house number, a
  name tag you can read, a document on the table.

## We can say no, and we do not have to explain why

Rejection is at our discretion and there is no appeal. A photo can be perfectly innocent
and still be turned down — most often simply because of the volume of photos we are
getting and the number of spots on the wall, or because it does not read well at the
size the lobby TV displays it.

Being rejected is not an accusation, and it is not a verdict on your cat. You are
welcome to send a different photo another time.

## What happens to a photo you send us

- It goes into a review queue, where someone from the app team looks at it.
- If we accept it, it may be displayed inside Puking Cat to other players, on the lobby
  TV and anywhere else in the game that shows featured cats.
- It may appear alongside the cat's name, as you typed it. Do not use a person's name.
- We may resize, crop, or compress it so it fits the screen.
- We may remove it at any time, for any reason, without telling you first.
- We do not sell it, and we do not use it in paid advertising.

If you would rather your cat did not appear in the game, the simplest thing is not to
submit the photo.

## Removing a photo that is already in the game

Email **info@happycode.studio** with the cat's name and, if you have it, the date you
submitted. Ask us to remove it. We will take it down — you do not have to give a reason
and we will not ask for one.

## Reporting a photo somebody else submitted

If you see a cat in the game that breaks these rules, or that you believe is your photo
used without permission, email **info@happycode.studio** and tell us what you saw. We
read that address and we act on reports about published photos before anything else in
the inbox.

If a photo is yours and was submitted by someone else, say so and we will remove it.

## Repeated problems

If someone keeps sending photos that break these rules, we stop accepting submissions
from them.

## Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines from time to time. The current version will always be
posted on this page with its effective date, and it is the version that applies.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about these Guidelines, or you want a photo removed, please
contact us at [info@happycode.studio](mailto:info@happycode.studio).
